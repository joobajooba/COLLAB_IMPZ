## Update (19:40): resources off-chain, crafted items on-chain
- **D-036:** nuggets, ingots and other resources live only in the database. They are not NFTs. This replaces the resource part of D-010, and D-019 and D-034.
- **D-037:** only finished crafted items are NFTs, as an open edition (many copies of each), sellable on OpenSea.
- **D-038:** crafting into NFTs is open for a limited time, then the collection is locked so supply is fixed.
- **What this improves:** no transaction fees for mining or smelting, a much simpler system, and resource supply no longer affects market prices.
- **New things to settle:** (1) the lock must be enforced by the contract, not just promised, because the contract is upgradeable; (2) what new players do after the lock; (3) who pays the minting fee; (4) bots will push hardest during the crafting window.
